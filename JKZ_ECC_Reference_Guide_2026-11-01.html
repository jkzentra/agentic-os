📖 How to use ECC

Three kinds of things. Same plugin, different invocation.

1 · CommandsType /ecc:plan style names. Action-oriented workflows (build-fix, code-review, pr…).
2 · SkillsKnowledge packs Claude loads on demand (patterns, testing, ops). Ask in plain words or say "use the ecc:X skill".
3 · AgentsSpecialist reviewers/resolvers Claude delegates to. Ask: "have the ecc:python-reviewer check this".
4 · CostEvery installed skill adds description text to context on every chat. Trim what you don't use.
