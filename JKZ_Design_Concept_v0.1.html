4 Template-variation rules

Protects YouTube monetisation under the "inauthentic content" policy: faceless is fine, repeated templates are not. The check (similarity.py) scores each pair: 0.4 hook words + 0.4 structure + 0.2 length. A score of 0.8 or more, or the same angle, blocks the later plan and sends it back for a new angle.

Stays fixed (the brand shell)

  • Palette, type, wordmark position, AI label.
  • Caption bar style and safe margins.
  • The 8-slide carousel skeleton roles.

Must change every piece

  • Hook: a different opening pattern (number, question, claim, story, contrast). No hook pattern twice in a week.
  • Structure: shorts rotate between 4 shapes: list, story, before/after, one-rule. Never the same shape back to back.
  • Length: spread across at least 3 bands (about 25 to 35 s, 40 to 55 s, 60 s+). No two within 15% of each other.
  • Angle: one real angle per piece, written in the brief. Same angle twice = blocked.
  • Script: original wording per piece, drafted from that week's brief, never a nouns-swapped copy.
  • Visual: alternate light and dark variants; vary the lead graphic.

Worked example (illustrative, not scored)

Would block: "3 mistakes that break your booking follow-up" and "3 mistakes that break your lead form": same hook words, same 5-step structure, same 45 s.

Would pass: the first, plus "We rebuilt a booking flow in one afternoon" (story, 38 s), plus "Is your auto-reply promising too much?" (check, 52 s).

5 Do and don't

Do

  • Write as "we" or "the studio". Direct, warm, short.
  • Show the result, never the receipt.
  • Label every piece "AI-generated". Disclose affiliate links on every GHL video.
  • Use real facts from the facts file; if a fact is missing, ask or flag.
  • One idea, one orange element, one call to action.
  • Reply to comments from approved templates only.
  • Keep USD for the first-tier lane.

Don't

  • Say "I", "my", or sound like a solo operator.
  • Name production tools in client-facing material (GHL is the disclosed exception).
  • Promise outcomes: "guaranteed", "10x", "double your", "overnight".
  • Use filler words: innovative, disruptive, best-in-class, synergy, leverage, "we try", "we hope".
  • Show RM prices anywhere a foreign-lane viewer can reach.
  • Name clients without written consent, name competitors, or touch politics, religion or personal finance advice.
  • Use faces, avatars, stock people or paid image generation in v0.1.
  • Post twice from the same template in one week.
